# Kestrel Home Co.: Returns and Remedies Policy (fictional, for testing)

**s1 Change of mind.** Unused items in their original packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund or store credit. The customer pays return postage. Clearance items are excluded. Change-of-mind returns are a store policy, not a legal entitlement.

**s2 Faulty, damaged or not as described.** These are handled under the Australian Consumer Law (ACL) consumer guarantees (see summary below). These rights are **not** limited by the 30-day change-of-mind window.

**s3 Damaged in transit.** The customer reports the damage, with photos if available. We offer a replacement or a refund. Customers do not need to return broken glass or ceramics.

**s4 Product safety.** Any report of sparking, burning, overheating, electric shock, smoke or injury is escalated to the Product Safety Lead within 2 business hours. Advise the customer to stop using the item immediately. Do not debate fault with the customer.

**s5 Late delivery.** If an order is 5 or more business days past its estimated delivery date, open a carrier investigation and refund the shipping fee. If the parcel is confirmed lost, offer a replacement or a full refund.

**s6 Approvals.** Refunds over $300 and any goodwill credit over $50 require Team Lead approval.

**s7 Payment security.** Staff must never ask for or record full card numbers. Billing disputes go to the Finance queue.

## ACL summary for the AI (plain English, based on ACCC guidance)

- Consumer guarantees apply automatically, including to online purchases.
- If a product has a **major** problem, the **customer** chooses a refund or a replacement of the same type.
- If the problem is **minor**, the **business** may choose to repair, replace or refund, within a reasonable time.
- A store cannot refuse a refund for a major failure (e.g. a "no refunds" sign is unlawful).
- Consumer guarantees do **not** cover change of mind.
- Whether a problem is major or minor depends on the circumstances. If unclear, a human must decide.

Source basis: ACCC (n.d.), *Repair, replace, refund, cancel*; ACCC (n.d.), *What if a consumer guarantee is not met*.
